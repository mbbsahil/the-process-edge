TRIZ Inventive Toolkit

TRIZ says most hard problems are contradictions: improving one thing makes another worse. Instead of compromising, use the 40 Inventive Principles to find a solution that removes the trade-off. Works for manufacturing, finance, services, healthcare, software and more.

1Describe the problem

One or two sentences. What is happening, where, and why it matters.

2State the contradiction

If I improve this… then that gets worse.

Now map each side to one of Altshuller's 39 engineering parameters. Business meanings are shown to help.

3Choose principles to try

Use the matrix suggestions, pick a few yourself, or get five at random. Random prompts often spark the best ideas.

4Generate ideas

For each principle, ask: “How could this idea remove my contradiction?” Write anything — judge later.

Select principles in step 3 to start.

5Save your worksheet

Stuck? Get a random principle and apply it to your problem in 2 minutes.

The 40 Inventive Principles were developed by Genrich Altshuller as part of TRIZ (Theory of Inventive Problem Solving), together with the 39×39 Contradiction Matrix used in step 2. Business examples by Sahil Anand · Lean Six Sigma Master Black Belt · Need help applying TRIZ? Get in touch.
